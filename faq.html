Frequently Asked Questions

What is MIRA?

MIRA is a fully automated crypto signal service that uses algorithmic trend-following. It sends instant buy and sell signals directly to your Telegram based on robust technical indicators.

How much does it cost?

MIRA offers a free tier allowing you to track 1 coin forever. Paid tiers unlock more coins starting at $9.99 per quarter.

Does MIRA trade for me?

No. The public MIRA Signal Service only sends Telegram alerts. You retain full control of your capital and execute the trades manually on your exchange.

What happens if I cancel?

If you cancel your subscription, you are downgraded to the Free tier at the end of your billing cycle, and you keep tracking your oldest selected coin.
